Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-07

This privacy policy applies to the Playko: Bricks and Balls mobile application (the "Application") created by furionfurion (the "Service Provider") as a free, ad-supported service. It is provided "AS IS".

Information we collect

The Application itself does not require registration and does not ask you to provide personal information directly. However, the Application includes third-party software development kits (SDKs) that automatically collect certain data when you use it:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID);
  • Approximate location (coarse, derived from your IP address);
  • App activity (gameplay events, sessions, interactions, in-app purchases);
  • Diagnostics (crash logs, performance data, device model and OS version).

Third-party services

The following partners process the data above to provide advertising and analytics:

  • Google AdMob — advertising;
  • Google Firebase — analytics and crash reporting;
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (processed by Google; we never see your payment details).

Advertising and consent (GDPR / EEA & UK)

In the European Economic Area and the UK, the Application shows a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before serving ads. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising; if you decline, ads will be non-personalized. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from the consent options. Ad content in the Application is limited to a General Audiences rating.

Your rights and opt-out

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads);
  • Opt out of personalized ads via the in-app consent form or device settings;
  • Request access to or deletion of data processed by the Service Provider by emailing furionforreal@gmail.com;
  • Stop all collection by uninstalling the Application.

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Depending on your country, you must be at least 16 years of age to consent to the processing of personal data, or have a parent or guardian consent on your behalf. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Application, please contact the Service Provider so it can be removed.

Data retention and security

Data collected by the third-party SDKs is retained according to the partners' policies linked above. Data is encrypted in transit. The Service Provider does not operate its own servers storing your personal data.
