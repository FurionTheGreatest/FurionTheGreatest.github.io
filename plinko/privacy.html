Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-08

This privacy policy applies to the Plinko Royale mobile game for Android (package com.furionfurion.plinkoroyale, the "Application") created by furionfurion (the "Service Provider"). The Application is free to play, supported by ads, and offers optional in-app purchases. It is provided "AS IS".

A game for adults, with no real money

The Application is a social casino-style game intended only for adults aged 18 and over. It does not offer real-money gambling or any opportunity to win real money or prizes. Chips and gems are virtual items with no cash value: they cannot be exchanged for money, withdrawn or transferred. Success in the Application does not imply future success at real-money gambling.

Information stored only on your device

The Application does not require registration, has no user accounts and does not ask you to enter personal information. Your game progress (chip and gem balances, level, unlocked boards, bonuses and timers), your purchase status (such as Remove Ads), a locally generated referral code and a locally generated player ID and name are stored only on your device. In version 1.0, scores are not sent to any server. Uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage deletes this data.

Information collected automatically

The Application includes third-party software development kits (SDKs) that automatically collect the following data when you use it:

  • Device or other identifiers — the Google Advertising ID and app set ID (Google AdMob), the Firebase app-instance ID (Google Analytics for Firebase) and an installation ID of the Unity In-App Purchasing library;
  • Approximate location — coarse location estimated from your IP address. The Application does not request access to your precise location;
  • App interactions — ad views, taps and video views (Google AdMob) and gameplay events such as sessions, rounds played, level-ups, rewarded videos watched and share-button taps (Google Analytics for Firebase);
  • Purchase history — when you make an in-app purchase: the product, price and transaction details (Google Analytics for Firebase and Unity In-App Purchasing);
  • Diagnostics — performance data of the Application and the SDKs, device model, operating system version and language.

Third-party services

The following partners process the data above on the Service Provider's behalf or for their own stated purposes:

  • Google AdMob — advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention;
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — analytics;
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases (gem packs and Remove Ads). Payments are processed by Google; the Service Provider never sees your payment details;
  • Unity Technologies — the game engine and the Unity In-App Purchasing library, which sends purchase transaction details and basic device information (model, language, app version) to Unity for purchase processing and analytics.

Game settings are downloaded from a public file on GitHub Pages (furionthegreatest.github.io). The request carries no identifiers or game data; as with any website, GitHub may log the IP address under its privacy statement.

Advertising

The Application shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, which may use the Advertising ID to show personalized ads. Ads for real-money gambling are blocked. Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and interstitial ads; rewarded videos remain optional. The Application is not distributed in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some other countries, and it does not display an ad consent form.

Your rights and choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads);
  • Delete the data stored on your device by clearing the Application's storage or uninstalling it;
  • Stop all collection by uninstalling the Application;
  • Ask questions about your data or request its deletion by emailing furionforreal@gmail.com. Because the Application has no accounts, the data collected by the SDKs is linked to device identifiers, not to your name, and the Service Provider cannot look it up by email. The Service Provider will answer your request and explain what can be done.

Data retention and security

Data collected by the third-party SDKs is retained according to the partners' policies linked above. All data is encrypted in transit. The Service Provider does not operate its own servers that store your personal data.
